Read this first: what "simulated customers" really means

The 10,000 customers are imagined by Claude, based on your website and your audience. They are not real people, and the scores are predictions, not real results. They're very good for one job: deciding which few ads deserve real money first, instead of paying to test all ten. Always confirm the winners with a small real test before you put a big budget behind them.

Part 1: make and test your ads

1

Create your Dreamina account

5 minutes
  1. Go to dreamina.capcut.com and sign up (Google or email works).
  2. Open your account and check how many credits you have. You'll need credits for 10 images.
  3. If you want a paid plan, open the pricing page and pick one. Look for current deals like the Black Friday offer.
2

Install the Dreamina CLI and connect it to Claude

10 minutes · Claude does most of it

The Dreamina CLI is a small program that lets Claude use Dreamina for you: it can make images and videos, check your credits and download the results. When it installs, it also adds an instruction file (a skill) that teaches AI assistants like Claude how to use it.

  1. Open Dreamina's official CLI page: dreamina.capcut.com/tools/dreamina-cli. It links to the official install page with the command to copy.
  2. Create a folder on your computer called My Ads.
  3. In the Claude desktop app, open the Code tab, start a Local session and choose that folder.
  4. Paste this prompt, with the install command from the official page:
COPY THIS PROMPTInstall the Dreamina CLI
I'm a beginner. Help me install the Dreamina CLI on this computer.
Here is the install command from Dreamina's official install page:
PASTE THE COMMAND FROM THE OFFICIAL PAGE

1. Check if it works on my computer (Windows / Mac). If my system
   needs something extra first, explain it simply and set it up.
2. Run the install and tell me when it's done.
3. When it's time to log in, tell me exactly what to do. I'll do
   the login myself in my browser.
4. Then check it works: show me the version and my credits.
5. Read the Dreamina skill file it installed so you know every
   command and option. Tell me in 3 lines what you can now do.
Do the login yourself

Dreamina's own page says to finish the login yourself rather than letting the AI do it. When the login page opens, sign in with your Dreamina account in your browser, then come back to Claude and say "done".

3

Get your folder ready

5 minutes

Put these in your My Ads folder:

  • Your product photo, named simply, like product.jpg. You can add 2–3 more angles if you have them.
  • Your logo (optional), so ads can include it.
  • A short brand note (optional): your brand colours, words you always use, words you never use, and who your customer is.
4

Prompt 1: make and test 10 ads

15–30 minutes · Claude works on its own

This is the prompt from the reel, with a few extra lines so it works smoothly for beginners. Paste it into the same Claude Code session:

COPY THIS PROMPTPrompt 1 · Make and test
You are my performance-marketing team.
My website: YOUR WEBSITE LINK
My product photo: product.jpg (in this folder)
I sell in: e.g. Lebanon, UAE, Saudi Arabia

1. Read my website: pull the offer, audience, brand voice and
   top 3 selling points.
2. Use the Dreamina CLI to turn this product photo into 10 ads:
   5 hooks × 2 styles, 9:16. Keep the product looking exactly
   like the real one. Save them in a folder called ads/.
3. Simulate 10,000 customers that match my real audience. Show
   every ad to all of them.
4. Score each ad: scroll-stop rate, click intent, purchase
   intent, top objection.
5. Rank them in a table. Name the 3 winners, say why they won,
   fix the losers.

Before you spend credits, tell me roughly how many this will
use and wait for my OK. Remind me the scores are predictions.
What "5 hooks × 2 styles" means

A hook is the first message people see, like "Glowing skin in 7 days" or "Stop wasting money on serums". A style is the look, like a clean studio shot or a real-life lifestyle scene. 5 hooks in 2 styles gives you 10 different ads, so you learn both which message and which look work best.

5

Read your results and pick the winners

10 minutes

Claude gives you a table like this. Here's what each score means:

ScoreWhat it tells you
Scroll-stop rateHow many people would stop scrolling to look. This is your hook and your image.
Click intentHow many would tap to learn more. This is your message and your offer.
Purchase intentHow many would actually buy. An ad can stop people and still not sell. This is the score that matters most.
Top objectionThe main reason people wouldn't buy, like "too expensive" or "does it really work?". Use it to improve the ad and your product page.

Look at each ad yourself before you trust the table. Check that the product looks real, the text has no spelling mistakes, and nothing promises something your product can't do. If something's wrong, just say: "Ad 4 changed the bottle shape. Remake it with the real product."

Part 2: resize and translate the winners

Now Claude opens Dreamina's Marketing Studio in Chrome. It's Dreamina's tool for product ads. It can resize images and videos for different formats, translate the text inside them, and make versions in different languages.

6

Connect Claude to Chrome

5 minutes
  1. In Google Chrome, open the Chrome Web Store and install the official Claude extension (Claude in Chrome), made by Anthropic.
  2. Sign in with the same Claude account you use in the desktop app, and follow the setup steps it shows.
  3. In Chrome, open dreamina.capcut.com and make sure you're signed in to Dreamina.
Stay in control

Claude works in your real browser, with your Dreamina account. Watch the first few steps, and stop it any time. It should never buy credits or change your account without asking you.

7

Prompt 2: every platform, every country

20–40 minutes · Claude works on its own
COPY THIS PROMPTPrompt 2 · Resize and localize
Open Dreamina's Marketing Studio in Chrome and take the 3 winning ads.

1. Resize each ad for every platform: Reels, Stories, TikTok,
   Shorts (9:16), Feed (4:5), Facebook (1:1). Reframe the
   product, don't just crop.
2. Localize each ad for these countries: YOUR MARKETS.
   Translate the hook, switch currency and CTA, keep my brand voice.
3. Export every version named country_platform_size, plus a
   launch checklist.

Ask me before you spend credits or download anything.

Replace YOUR MARKETS with your countries, for example: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Lebanon, France.

ShapeSize (pixels)Where it's used
9:16 (tall)1080 × 1920Instagram Reels and Stories, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
4:5 (portrait)1080 × 1350Instagram and Facebook feed
1:1 (square)1080 × 1080Facebook feed and right-column placements
Check every translation

AI translations can sound strange or miss local slang. Ask someone who speaks the language to read each version. For Arabic, also check the text reads right to left and the prices use the right currency (AED, SAR, LBP or USD).

8

Launch the winners with a small real test

The step that makes it real
  1. In Meta Ads Manager (Instagram and Facebook) or TikTok Ads Manager, create one campaign with your 3 winning ads.
  2. Give each ad the same small budget for 3–5 days.
  3. Compare the real numbers: cost per click and cost per purchase.
  4. Put more money behind the real winner. Give the results to Claude and ask: "Here are my real results. Make 5 new versions of the winner."

Every round, your ads get better, and you spend money only on what's working.

Mini glossary

CLICommand line interface: a program you control by typing commands. Here, Claude types them for you.
CreditsWhat Dreamina charges for each image or video it makes.
SkillAn instruction file that teaches Claude how to use a tool.
HookThe first line or image that makes people stop scrolling.
CTACall to action: what you ask people to do, like "Shop now".
LocalizeAdapt an ad for a country: language, currency and local style.
Aspect ratioThe shape of an image or video, like 9:16 (tall) or 1:1 (square).
