What this guide will help you do

My story

I was training for Ironman 70.3 in Egypt, and I was struggling. Training every day, plus work, clients, content and studies. I didn't know what to eat or when. I kept forgetting to drink water. Every eating plan I made, I dropped after a week.

So I thought: I'm a developer, let me build something that helps. I made an app with a bot that learned from the best training and nutrition books. It schedules everything (training, food, work), reminds me to drink water every two hours, tells me what to eat each day based on that day's training, and tracks my health in one place. Now I don't have to think about it. The bot thinks for me.

If I built it for an Ironman, you can build it for any goal.

By the end of this guide you'll have your own AI coach. It knows your goal, your schedule and your limits. It plans your week, tells you what to do today, adjusts when life gets in the way, and keeps you on track with reminders. It works for a marathon, losing weight, studying for exams, growing a business, or anything else you're working towards.

Read this first: health and safety

Your AI coach is a planning helper, not a doctor or a dietitian, and AI can be wrong. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before big changes to your training or diet, especially for endurance events, if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, take medication, or have a history of eating problems. If something hurts or feels wrong, stop and get real medical advice.

Two ways to build it

Path A: no code

A Claude Project that remembers your goal, plans your days and answers your check-ins. Reminders come from your phone. About 30 minutes to set up. Start here.

Path B: build an app or bot

Use Claude Code to build a small app or a Telegram bot that sends you reminders, logs your water, food and training, and makes your daily plan. More setup, more automatic.

You can start with Path A today and move to Path B later. Your goal sheet works for both.

1

Write your goal sheet

15 minutes · the most important step

Your coach is only as good as what it knows about you. Open a note and fill this in honestly:

FILL THIS INGoal sheet
MY GOAL: e.g. finish Ironman 70.3 / run 10 km / lose 6 kg / pass my exam
DEADLINE: DATE
WHERE I AM NOW: e.g. I can run 5 km, I weigh 82 kg, I've studied 2 of 10 chapters

MY WEEK:
- Work / study hours: e.g. Mon-Fri 9-5
- Fixed commitments: e.g. clients Tue/Thu evenings
- Best time to train or focus: e.g. 6am
- Sleep: e.g. 11pm-6:30am

FOOD:
- Likes / dislikes: LIST
- Allergies or restrictions: LIST
- Cooking time per day: e.g. 30 min
- Budget: e.g. normal supermarket food

MY HEALTH NOTES (from my doctor): ANYTHING IMPORTANT
WHY I KEEP FAILING: e.g. I forget, I get bored, I'm too tired after work
HOW I WANT TO BE COACHED: e.g. short, direct, a bit of tough love

Path A: your coach in Claude (no code)

2

Create your coach

10 minutes
  1. Open claude.ai or the Claude app and go to Projects. Create a new project called My Coach.
  2. Open the project's instructions and paste the coach instructions below, with your goal sheet at the end.
  3. Add your knowledge files to the project: your goal sheet, any training plan your coach or doctor gave you, your own notes, and books or guides you own and have the right to use. Use them for yourself only.
COPY INTO PROJECT INSTRUCTIONSCoach instructions
You are my personal coach for the goal in my goal sheet below.

How you work:
- Plan my week around my real schedule, work and sleep.
- Every day, tell me: today's training or focus task, what to
  eat (based on today's training or workload), when to drink
  water, and one small habit to keep.
- Base advice on the knowledge files in this project and on
  well-established guidance. If you're not sure, say so.
- If I miss a day, don't lecture me. Adjust the plan and
  keep going.
- Keep messages short and practical. Use simple English.
- Never give medical advice. If I mention pain, illness,
  dizziness or anything worrying, tell me to stop and see a
  professional.

MY GOAL SHEET:
PASTE YOUR GOAL SHEET HERE
3

Daily and weekly check-ins

2 minutes a day

Open a new chat inside your project each morning and send your check-in. Once a week, ask for the next week's plan.

COPY THIS PROMPTEvery morning
Morning check-in.
Sleep: HOURS · Energy: 1-10 · Anything sore: NO / WHAT
Yesterday I did: WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DID
Today I have: e.g. work 9-5, clients at 7pm
Give me today's plan: training or focus task, meals and snacks with times, water reminders, and one tip.
COPY THIS PROMPTEvery Sunday
Weekly review. This week I managed: WHAT WENT WELL / WHAT I MISSED
Next week's commitments: LIST
Plan my next 7 days in a table (day, training or focus, meals, key tasks). Make it slightly harder than this week only if I did well. Otherwise keep it the same or easier.
4

Food plans that match your day

The thing I struggled with most

A hard training day and a rest day need different food. Ask your coach to plan both, then it picks the right one each morning:

COPY THIS PROMPTMeal templates
Make me 3 simple day templates of meals I actually like: a hard training day, an easy training day, and a rest day. For each: meal times, what to eat, roughly how much, and pre/post-training snacks. Use only foods from my goal sheet, and keep cooking under 30 minutes. Then give me one weekly shopping list.
5

Put it in your calendar, and set reminders

10 minutes
  • Calendar: ask "Turn this week's plan into a calendar file (.ics) I can import." Download the file and open it with Google Calendar or your phone's calendar. If your Claude account has a calendar connector, it may be able to add events directly. Check every event before you save.
  • Water every 2 hours: on iPhone, use the Reminders app or a Shortcuts automation. On Android, use Google Tasks or Clock alarms that repeat every day at set times (for example 9:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00, 19:00).
  • Morning check-in: set one daily reminder: "Open My Coach and check in".

Path B: build a coach app or bot

This is closer to what I built for myself: the coach messages you, instead of you remembering to open it. You don't need to know how to code. Claude Code writes and runs it, and you describe what you want.

What you need

You needDetails
Claude CodeThe Code tab in the Claude desktop app (paid Claude plans).
Your goal sheetFrom Step 1, saved as a text file.
Telegram (for the bot version)Free. You'll create a bot with Telegram's official @BotFather, which gives you a bot token. Keep that token secret.
A computer that stays on, or a small serverFor reminders to arrive on time. Claude can explain your options, including free tiers.
6

Build it with one prompt

1–2 hours, mostly Claude working
  1. Create a folder called My Coach App and put your goal sheet inside it.
  2. In the Claude desktop app, open the Code tab, start a Local session, and choose that folder.
  3. Paste this prompt:
COPY THIS PROMPTBuild my coach
I'm a beginner. Build me a personal coach as a Telegram bot / simple phone web app, using my goal sheet in this folder.

It should:
1. Every morning at 6:30, send me today's plan: training or focus task,
   meals for that type of day, and one habit.
2. Remind me to drink water every 2 hours from 9:00 to 21:00.
3. Let me log water, meals, training and how I feel with short
   messages (like "water 500" or "ran 5k easy").
4. Every Sunday, send a weekly summary and next week's plan.
5. Adjust the plan if I miss days, without guilt-tripping me.
6. Keep my data private: store it on my computer only, keep
   secrets (like the bot token) in a settings file that never
   gets shared, and tell me where everything is stored.

Explain each step in one simple sentence before you do it.
Tell me exactly when I need to create an account or paste a
token, and where to paste it. Never ask me to paste secrets
in this chat. At the end, show me how to start it, stop it,
and change the reminder times.
Start small

Get the water reminders and morning plan working first. Add logging and the weekly summary once those work. Small steps make problems much easier to fix.

7

Optional: connect your watch or health app

For training data

If you use Apple Health, Garmin or Strava, most of them let you export your workouts as files. Put an export in your folder and ask: "Read this export and use my real training load when planning food and rest days." Ask Claude to explain how to export from your specific app, and only connect accounts you're comfortable sharing with your own app.

Fixes

ProblemWhat to do
The plan doesn't fit my real lifeUpdate your goal sheet with your real schedule and say: "This plan is too much. Make it fit my actual week."
The coach forgets thingsKeep important details in the project instructions or knowledge files, not only in old chats.
I stopped after a week (again)Tell the coach why. Ask for a smaller, easier plan for 2 weeks. Consistency beats intensity.
Reminders don't arrive (Path B)Your computer may be asleep. Ask Claude Code how to keep it running, or how to move it to a small server.
Food advice feels offCheck it with a registered dietitian and add their guidance to your knowledge files.

Your checklist

  • I wrote an honest goal sheet
  • I created a "My Coach" project with the instructions and my files
  • I did my first morning check-in and got a plan for today
  • I have meal templates for hard, easy and rest days
  • My plan is in my calendar and my water reminders are set
  • (Path B) My bot or app sends my morning plan and water reminders
  • I checked big changes with a doctor or dietitian

Mini glossary

Claude ProjectA space in Claude with its own instructions and files that every chat inside it can use.
Knowledge filesDocuments you add to a project so Claude can use them in its answers.
Telegram botAn automated account on Telegram that can send you messages and reply to you.
Web app (PWA)A website you can add to your phone's home screen so it works like an app.
.ics fileA calendar file you can import into Google Calendar or your phone.
TokenA secret key that lets your app use a service. Never share it.
