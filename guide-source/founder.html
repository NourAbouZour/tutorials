The basics

What is a Claude "skill"?

Think of Claude as a very smart assistant who knows a little about everything. A skill is like handing that assistant a detailed instruction folder for one specific job: "here is exactly how a CFO checks a business, step by step."

Once a skill is installed, Claude reads that folder whenever your request matches the job. You don't need to remember any special commands. You just talk to Claude normally, and it follows the expert instructions in the background.

Good to know

A skill is just a set of text files (instructions, and sometimes small helper scripts). It doesn't cost anything to run on its own. You only use your normal Claude plan.

The pack

What's in Founder Skill

Founder Skill isn't one skill. It's a pack of 11 skills, and each one plays a different role you would normally have to hire for. These are the four you'll use most:

Strategy

Board of directors

Reviews your idea the way famous founders and investors think, in the style of Alex Hormozi (offers and value), Peter Thiel (being different, not just better) and Steve Jobs (product and simplicity).

Growth

Marketing director

Works out who exactly your customer is, what message will grab them, and which channels to use to reach them first.

Money

CFO

Checks your prices and costs, finds your profit margin, and tells you where you're losing money and how much you need to sell to break even.

The best one

Consumer skill

Creates 100 AI "customers", each with a different budget, objection and buying style, and runs your business past every one of them.

The other skills in the pack cover the remaining parts of starting a business. You don't need to memorise them. Once it's installed, Claude can list them for you (you'll do that in Step 2).

1

Install the skill

About 5 minutes · choose Option A or B

Option A (easiest, the way I showed in the reel): the Code tab

  1. Open the Claude desktop app and sign in.
  2. Click the Code tab at the top.
  3. Start a new session. If it asks where to work, choose Local and pick any folder, for example a new empty folder called My Business in Documents.
  4. Paste the prompt below, with the Founder Skill link in place of the yellow text, and press Enter.
  5. If Claude asks for permission to download or create files, click Allow. It's only fetching the skill's files.
COPY THIS PROMPTInstall
Install skill: PASTE THE FOUNDER SKILL LINK HERE

Install it for my user account so it works in every project, not only this folder. When you're done, tell me in simple words where you installed it and whether I need to restart anything.

Option B: upload it in Claude's settings

  1. Open the Founder Skill link in your browser. If it's on GitHub, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP.
  2. In Claude (web or app), open Settings, then Capabilities.
  3. Make sure code execution / file creation is switched on. Skills need it.
  4. In the Skills section, choose Upload skill and select the ZIP file. If the pack contains several skill folders, you may need to upload each one as its own ZIP.
Menus move around

Claude's app updates often, so a button might have a slightly different name than in this guide. If you can't find something, ask Claude itself: "Where do I upload a skill in this app?"

Using it

Put your business through the test

Do the next steps in order, in the same chat. Each step builds on what Claude learned in the step before, so you only explain your business once.

3

Describe your business

5 minutes · the most important step

The better Claude understands your idea, the better every answer after this will be. Fill in what you know and write "not sure" for the rest. That's fine.

COPY THIS PROMPTYour business brief
I want to use Founder Skill to test a business before I spend money on it. Here is my idea:

What I sell: e.g. custom birthday cakes
Who it's for: e.g. parents in Beirut planning kids' parties
Price: e.g. $40 per cake
My costs (roughly): e.g. $15 ingredients + $5 box and delivery
How I'll sell it: e.g. Instagram and WhatsApp orders
My budget to start: e.g. $300
My goal: e.g. 30 orders a month within 3 months

Before we start, ask me up to 5 questions about anything important that's missing. Then summarise my business in 5 bullet points so I can confirm you understood it.

Answer Claude's questions, check its summary, and correct anything that's wrong before moving on.

4

Hold a board of directors meeting

5 minutes

Real founders have a board: experienced people who challenge their decisions. Now you have one too.

COPY THIS PROMPTBoard meeting
Use the board of directors skill. Hold a board meeting about my business.

Let each board member (thinking like Hormozi, Peter Thiel and Steve Jobs) give me:
1. Their honest opinion of the idea
2. The single biggest risk they see
3. One change they would make

Then finish with the board's final vote: go, change it, or stop. Explain the vote in simple words.
Push back

Don't just accept the verdict. If you disagree, say so: "I think the price is fine because... Does the board still agree?" Arguing with your board is how you find the weak spots.

6

Let the CFO check the money

5 minutes

A CFO (Chief Financial Officer) is the person in a company who looks after the money. The most important number they'll give you is your profit margin: how much of each sale you actually keep.

Simple exampleAmount
You sell a cake for$40
Ingredients + box + delivery cost$20
Profit per cake$40 − $20 = $20
Profit margin$20 ÷ $40 = 50% (you keep half of every sale)
COPY THIS PROMPTCFO check
Use the CFO skill. Check the money side of my business:

1. My profit per sale and my profit margin, showing the maths
2. Costs I might be forgetting (fees, packaging, delivery, ads, my own time)
3. How many sales I need each month to break even, and to make $1,000 profit
4. Where I'm losing margin, and 3 ways to improve it
5. Is my price too low, too high or right? Why?

Put the numbers in a simple table.
Check the maths

AI can occasionally make calculation mistakes. For any number you'll base a real decision on, ask Claude to "show the calculation step by step" and check it with a calculator.

7

The consumer test: 100 customers

10 minutes · the best part

This is the skill that makes Founder Skill special. Claude creates 100 different AI customers, each with their own budget, objections and way of deciding to buy, and shows your business to every one of them. You get a preview of how the market might react before you open.

COPY THIS PROMPT100 customers
Use the consumer skill. Run my business past 100 simulated customers who match my target market, with a realistic mix of budgets, objections and buying styles.

Then give me:
1. How many would buy, how many might buy, and how many wouldn't
2. The top 5 reasons people said yes
3. The top 5 objections (reasons people said no), ranked by how often they came up
4. 3 short quotes from the most interesting customers
5. The 3 changes that would turn the most "no"s into "yes"es

How to read the results

  • Lots of "no" for the same reason? That's gold. It's the biggest problem to fix before you launch.
  • The "maybe" group is where you can grow fastest. Ask Claude: "What would make the 'maybe' customers say yes?"
  • Change one thing and test again. For example: "Run the 100 customers again, but this time the price is $35 and delivery is free." Compare the two results.
Simulated customers aren't real customers

The 100 customers are an educated guess, not a survey. Use them to find problems early and cheaply. Then confirm the most important findings with 5–10 real people in your target market before you spend real money.

8

Turn it all into a launch plan

5 minutes

Now combine everything into one plan you can actually follow.

COPY THIS PROMPTLaunch plan
Using everything from this chat (the board meeting, marketing plan, CFO numbers and the 100-customer test), write my launch plan:

1. The changes I should make to my idea before launching
2. A 30-day launch plan, week by week, with simple daily tasks
3. How I'll spend my $300 budget, line by line
4. The 3 numbers I should track every week to know if it's working
5. A short list of things I should NOT spend money on yet

Write it so a complete beginner can follow it.
Keep it

Ask Claude: "Put the full launch plan in a document I can download." Then save it and look at it every week.

Before you launch

Your checklist

  • Founder Skill is installed and Claude can list all 11 skills
  • I described my business, answered Claude's questions and confirmed its summary
  • I held a board meeting and wrote down the biggest risk
  • I have a clear ideal customer and a one-sentence message
  • I know my profit per sale, my margin and my break-even number (and checked the maths)
  • I ran the 100-customer test and know my top 3 objections
  • I changed my idea based on the results and tested again
  • I talked to 5–10 real people to confirm the biggest findings
  • I saved my 30-day launch plan
Words you'll see

Mini glossary

SkillA set of expert instructions Claude follows for a specific job.
AgentAn AI that plays a role and acts on its own, like one simulated customer.
Board of directorsExperienced advisers who challenge a company's big decisions.
CFOChief Financial Officer: the person responsible for a company's money.
Profit marginThe percentage of each sale you keep after costs. ($20 profit on a $40 sale = 50%.)
Break-evenThe number of sales where you've covered all your costs and start making profit.
ObjectionA reason a customer gives for not buying ("too expensive", "I don't trust it").
