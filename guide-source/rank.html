Keyword difficulty vs personal keyword difficulty

Keyword Difficulty (KD%)

How hard it is for any website to get into Google's top 10 for this keyword. The same number for everyone.

Personal Keyword Difficulty (PKD%)

How hard it is for your website. Semrush looks at how well the keyword matches your site's topic, how strong your site is, and how strong the sites already ranking are.

Why it matters: a keyword with KD 55% looks too hard. But if your site already covers that topic well, its PKD might be 25%: very doable for you. And the opposite happens too: a "low KD" keyword can be hard for you if it's far from your topic. PKD stops you wasting months on the wrong keywords.

Part 1: find your keywords

1

Set up Semrush

5 minutes
  1. Go to semrush.com and create an account.
  2. A free account lets you run a limited number of searches per day. Paid plans usually come with a free trial. Check the current offer on their pricing page, and set a reminder before a trial ends.
  3. Add your website as a project when Semrush asks. That makes the next steps faster.
2

Run your search in the Keyword Magic Tool

2 minutes
  1. In the left menu, open SEO → Keyword Magic Tool.
  2. Type the keyword you want to rank for. Start broad, for example dentist, protein powder or web design.
  3. Type your website in the domain field next to it (for example yourwebsite.com). This is the game changer: without your domain, you won't see personal difficulty.
  4. Choose the country where your customers are, then click Search.
One limitation

Semrush says these domain-based scores aren't available for local keyword research (searches for one city). Use the country-level database for this.

3

Read the results

5 minutes

You get a long list of related keywords. These are the columns that matter:

ColumnWhat it means
VolumeAbout how many times people search it each month in that country.
KD %General difficulty, for everyone.
PKD %Personal difficulty, for your site. This is the one you sort by.
IntentWhat the searcher wants: Informational (to learn), Navigational (a specific site), Commercial (to compare), Transactional (to buy).
CPCWhat advertisers pay per click. A high CPC usually means the keyword makes money.

As a rough guide to Semrush's difficulty scores: 0–29 is easy, 30–49 takes some work, 50–69 is hard, and 70+ is very hard.

4

Filter for your easy wins

10 minutes
  1. Sort or filter by PKD %, lowest first. Focus on keywords under about 30%.
  2. Remove keywords with almost no volume. Even 50–200 searches a month is worth it if they're the right people.
  3. Check the intent. If you sell something, the C and T keywords bring buyers. I keywords bring readers you can turn into buyers.
  4. Remove anything that doesn't fit what you actually offer.
  5. Select your best 20–50 and click Export to download them as a file.
The sweet spot

Low PKD + decent volume + the right intent. Leave the hard keywords for later, when your site is stronger. The easy wins make it stronger.

Part 2: turn keywords into traffic

5

Plan your pages with Claude

10 minutes

Open claude.ai, attach your exported file, and paste:

COPY THIS PROMPTKeyword plan
This is my keyword export from Semrush for my website YOURWEBSITE.COM.
I sell: WHAT YOU SELL, AND TO WHOM

1. Group keywords that mean the same thing (one page per group).
2. For each group, pick the main keyword and suggest the page:
   new page or improve an existing one, the page title and URL.
3. Rank the groups by: lowest PKD, then highest volume, then
   buying intent.
4. Give me a 4-week plan: which pages to write first.
Show it as a table.
6

Write a page that ranks

30–60 minutes per page

Before you write, search your keyword on Google and open the top 3 results. Your page needs to answer the question better than they do. Then ask Claude:

COPY THIS PROMPTWrite the page
Write a page for my website targeting the keyword "KEYWORD".
Other keywords from the same group: LIST.
Here's what the top 3 Google results cover: PASTE A SHORT SUMMARY OR THEIR HEADINGS

- Answer the searcher's question in the first 2 sentences.
- Cover everything the top results cover, plus what they miss.
- Use the keyword in the title, the first paragraph, one heading
  and the URL, naturally, without stuffing.
- Add an FAQ with 4 real questions people ask.
- Write a meta title (under 60 characters) and meta description
  (under 155 characters).
- My voice: e.g. friendly, expert, simple. Add a call to action to YOUR OFFER.

Then make it yours: add your own photos, real examples, prices and experience. Google rewards pages written by people who actually know the topic. Link to the new page from 2–3 of your existing pages.

7

Track your results

5 minutes a week
  • In Semrush, add your keywords to Position Tracking to see where you rank each day.
  • Connect your site to Google Search Console (free) to see real clicks and impressions from Google.
  • Be patient: new pages usually take 4–12 weeks to settle. If a page is stuck on page 2 after that, ask Claude to compare it with the top 3 results and improve it.

Fixes

ProblemWhat to do
I don't see the PKD % columnMake sure you typed your domain next to the keyword before clicking Search, and that you're not using a local (city) database.
All keywords have high PKDYour keyword is too broad. Try longer, more specific ones: "vegan protein powder for women" instead of "protein powder".
"You've reached your daily limit"Free accounts get a few searches per day. Plan your searches, or use a trial when you're ready to do it all at once.
Keywords don't match my businessUse the include and exclude filters in the Keyword Magic Tool to remove unrelated words.
My page isn't ranking after 3 monthsCheck it's indexed in Google Search Console, improve it against the top results, and get a few links to it from other sites.
