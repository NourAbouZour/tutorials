What Claude Motion is (and isn't)

What it is

Animated text, charts, shapes and images that move and play like a real video. Perfect for explainers, reports, walkthroughs and announcements.

What it isn't

Not an AI video generation model. No fake footage and no AI-generated people. If you need realistic scenes or a presenter, film them or use another tool, then combine them in an editor.

Because Claude writes the animation as code, every part of it is precise and editable. Ask for one change, and only that thing changes. With AI video, a small change usually means regenerating the whole clip and hoping it comes out the same.

Make your first video

1

Open Motion

1 minute

There are two ways in. Use whichever you see:

  • From a chat: start a new chat, click Output in the message box, and choose Motion. In the reel I type /motion. If your app offers that command, it does the same thing.
  • From the Artifacts tab: open the Artifacts tab and pick a Motion template.

Every animation you make is saved in the Artifacts tab, so you can come back and edit it later.

2

Drop in your file and say what you want

2 minutes

Attach the file you want to turn into a video: a report, a slide deck, a spreadsheet, a document, or some images. Then describe the video. The example from the reel:

COPY THIS PROMPTYour first video
Turn this report into a 30 second explainer for our team meeting.

That works. But the more you tell Claude, the better the first version. Use this formula:

FILL THIS INThe 5-part request
Turn this report / these slides / this data into a 30 second
animated explainer.

Who will watch it: e.g. our sales team / new customers / my followers
Where it will play: e.g. on a big screen in a meeting / Instagram Reels (9:16)
The one thing they should remember: e.g. revenue is up 42%
Style: e.g. clean and calm, our brand colours #6a4cf5 and white
Scenes: one idea per scene, short text, big numbers.
Say who, where and how long

Anthropic's own tip: tell Claude who will watch it, where it will play, and roughly how long it should be. Those three details change everything: the text size, the speed and the shape of the video.

3

Watch the first version

2 minutes

Play it from start to finish once, and check:

  • The numbers. Are they exactly right, compared with your file?
  • The text. Can you read every line before it disappears?
  • The order. Does the story make sense from scene to scene?
  • The ending. Does it end on the one thing people should remember?

Change one thing, keep everything else

4

Edit with plain English

Seconds per change

This is the magic part. Just say what to change:

To changeSay something like
One word"In scene 2, change 'growth' to 'record growth'."
One number"Change the Q3 number to 18%."
The timing"Slow down the second scene." or "Hold the final number for 2 more seconds."
The order"Swap scenes 3 and 4."
The look"Use our brand purple for every chart."
The shape"Make a 9:16 version for Instagram Reels."

You can also choose Edit directly and adjust the animation yourself in the editor.

Why this is so useful

Your report changes every month, but the video doesn't have to be rebuilt. Next month, attach the new numbers and say: "Update every number from this new report. Keep everything else the same."

5

Export as MP4

1 minute

When you're happy, click Export and choose MP4. That gives you a normal video file you can play anywhere, send on WhatsApp, upload to YouTube, or post on Instagram.

Before a big presentation, export once and play the file on the screen you'll actually use, to check the size and quality.

6

Finish it in CapCut or Runway (optional)

10 minutes

Claude Motion makes the visuals. An editor adds the rest:

  1. Open CapCut (free, on phone and computer) or Runway, and start a new project.
  2. Import your MP4.
  3. Add your voiceover, background music, and captions.
  4. Add real footage if you want: you on camera, your product, your team. Then export again.
7

Share it

Your choice

New animations are private to you. To show your team, open the animation and click Share. Or simply send the MP4.

Tips for great results

  • One idea per scene. If a scene needs two sentences, split it in two.
  • Fewer words, bigger text. People watch, they don't read.
  • Give exact brand colours and fonts, so every video looks like yours.
  • Start short. Get a great 20–30 second version, then make it longer if you need to.
  • Always check numbers against your file before you share.
